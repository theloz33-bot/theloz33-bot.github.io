About this hub

Omong Studio is one creator working with a small stack of AI tools. The work is vertical short-form series, music videos, brand films and single-file web tools, and everything published here comes out of a project that was actually finished.

Two journals carry the writing. The Korean journal, {{kr_name}}, covers household decisions and one-person production: which humidifier for which room, how to light a phone-shot interview, what to check in an AI video quote. The English journal, {{en_name}}, is the working notebook for AI pipelines — character consistency across episodes, cutting a music video against a locked mix, speech audio levels, and packaging a tool as a product.

Both journals are static sites. There are no accounts, no comment sections and no newsletter pop-ups. Where a link is an affiliate link it is labelled, and the disclosure page says which ones and why. The privacy policy explains what the host logs and what Google and other third-party vendors may set through advertising cookies.

If you want a video made rather than read about making one, the studio takes commissions for long-form films, 9:16 ad cuts and original brand songs. The contact page lists the working channels — a Korean marketplace storefront, the Gumroad shop and the public repository.
